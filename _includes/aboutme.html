I am a Ph.D. student in Biostatistics at the University of Washington, advised by Professor Eardi Lila. My research develops functional data analysis methods for neuroimaging and biomarkers in Alzheimer's disease. My broader interests include spatial and environmental statistics, functional data analysis, and network modeling.

I also work with Professor Amy Willis on phylogenetic modeling methods to assist allergen detection from pollen samples, and with Professor Jon Wakefield on Bayesian models of mortality data that address missingness to produce more robust estimates of child mortality.

I grew up between Seongnam, South Korea, and Madison, Wisconsin, and I am a proud Wisconsin Badger, with a B.S. in Statistics and certificates in Mathematics and Economic Analytics. I am a native speaker of English and Korean.
